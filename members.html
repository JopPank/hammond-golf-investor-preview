Playing together

Handicaps & course care.

The 2026 newsletter encouraged members to maintain a valid Golf Canada handicap for OVGA and most club competitions. It also reminded everyone to:

  • Replace divots.
  • Rake bunkers after use.
  • Repair ball marks on greens.
  • Keep carts off greens and outside treelines.
