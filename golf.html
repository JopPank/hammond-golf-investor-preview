About the course.

Our turf staff takes pride in keeping Hammond’s fairways and greens in great condition. Enjoy a fun, challenging round among mature pine trees, only about 25 minutes from Ottawa.

  • 18 holes, par 72
  • Five sets of tees
  • From 5,167 yards (red tees) to 6,626 yards (blue tees)
  • Mature pine trees

Rooted in Hammond

Our history.

The story begins with the Léonard family farm. Samuel Léonard and Obéline Séquin bought the property in 1884. Obéline built the distinctive red-brick family home in 1909, and the family farm and general store became a landmark in the community.

In the 1970s, the family began imagining a golf course on the land. Since opening as a course in 1980, Hammond has welcomed golfers to enjoy a challenging round in the countryside.
