
---

### 1. Immersive Visual Storytelling & Hero Media

* **What They Do Well:**
* *Gleneagles* immediately immerses visitors with high-end cinematic video and photography emphasizing "The Glorious Playground," blending luxury lifestyle, golf, and dining.
* *St Andrews* uses interactive 360° virtual hole-by-hole aerial tours and live webcams.


* **What Hammond Can Incorporate:**
* Feature high-production cinematic background video on the homepage highlighting the pristine fairways alongside drone shots of the **Broken Stick brewery** patio and **Hammond Hill** eco-glamping.
* Implement interactive course hole flyovers so prospective visitors can visualize the strategic layout.



### 2. Seamless "Stay, Play & Dine" Package Bundling

* **What They Do Well:**
* *Pinehurst* and *Gleneagles* excel at packaging their assets together (Golf + Accommodations + Dining + Spa) with clear, frictionless booking paths for multi-day trips and buddy groups.


* **What Hammond Can Incorporate:**
* Create dedicated, one-click landing pages for bundled packages (e.g., the *"Stay, Play & Brew"* weekend package combining golf, Hammond Hill glamping, and Broken Stick dining).
* Make booking an all-inclusive trip as effortless as booking a single tee time.



### 3. Clear Categorization for Diverse Audiences

* **What They Do Well:**
* Their navigation bars immediately segment users by intent: *Golfers* (tee times, course status, academy), *Guests/Stay* (cottages, hotels), *Dining*, and *Events/Weddings*.


* **What Hammond Can Incorporate:**
* Restructure the website navigation to cater directly to three distinct personas:
1. **Daily-Fee & Enthusiast Golfers** (Tee times, rates, memberships).
2. **Destination Tourists & Groups** (Stay-and-play packages, brewery/pub visitors).
3. **Event & Corporate Planners** (Tournaments, weddings, private pavilion bookings).





### 4. Interactive Course Status & Transparency

* **What They Do Well:**
* *St Andrews* and *Pinehurst* provide prominent, real-time updates regarding course conditions, weather alerts, and seasonal maintenance schedules right on the homepage.


* **What Hammond Can Incorporate:**
* Add a prominent "Course Status & Weather" widget on the homepage banner (Open/Cart Path Only/Closed, current temperature, and green speeds) to build trust and eliminate unnecessary phone calls to the pro shop.



### 5. Content Hubs: News, Blogs, and Social Proof

* **What They Do Well:**
* They feature dynamic journals or stories (e.g., Pinehurst's design updates, St Andrews' historic milestones) alongside integrated user-generated content (UGC) galleries featuring real guest photos (`#HomeOfGolf`).


* **What Hammond Can Incorporate:**
* Add a "Stories from the Course & Brewery" blog section highlighting course renovations, chef specials at the Dandy Lion, and new craft beer releases from Broken Stick.
* Embed a live Instagram/social wall showing real golfers enjoying the course and patio.
